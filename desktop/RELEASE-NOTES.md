## What is in the window

The whole street, the way the browser build has it — the city, the traffic, the water and the
boats, the pedestrians and the police, the radio, the turning day — in a real desktop frame that
starts in one double-click and asks for nothing else.

- **Run it:** unzip anywhere, run `Vice-86-Revanced.exe`. No installer, no runtime to add, no
  internet after the first start: every asset ships in the folder.
- **Why a desktop build runs smoother:** the page gets the machine's own GPU through Chromium's
  hardware path with no browser chrome, no tabs competing for frames, and a window that owns the
  screen. Pair it with the **Ultra High** preset if the card has room, or leave it on Auto.
- **Controls** are the page's: WASD or arrows to drive, F to get in and out, M for the radio,
  T for the hour, C for the camera. The full list is in the pause menu.

## What changed since the last build

- **Citizens, not creatures.** The pavement is walked by procedurally built modern people — six
  skin tones, ordinary clothes, hair and caps — rigged to the same skeleton the street already
  animates, so every clip fits them. The fantasy shelf is retired.
- **Police on foot.** While the record wants you, uniformed officers spawn at the crowd's edge and
  walk you down; an arrest is a fine, a wiped record and a walk back from the shore.
- **Ultra High.** A rung above Ultra: supersampled, 4096 px shadow cascades, 20-tap occlusion,
  full-res reflections, 1536 lights, 8192 particles. The crowd answers with 26 pedestrians.
- **The radio.** Three stations synthesised live — lofi, synthwave and a swung jazz combo — on the
  M key, with generated track titles. It plays only while there is a dashboard.
- **Running leans** into the stride, and **getting into a car is an arc**, not a cut: the body
  swings in around the door and the door sound splits open/shut across the sequence.
- **The day turns by default**, and every sound is re-rendered at 48 kHz.
